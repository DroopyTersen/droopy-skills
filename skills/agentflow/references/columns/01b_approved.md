# Approved

An eligible queue of work Andrew has decided deserves attention. Cards may still be raw and may have dependencies. There only needs to be enough context to start research.

When Andrew asks to start:

1. Use the named card, or the top unblocked card in the Approved column's visible order when he delegates selection.
2. Check dependencies and current ownership. Skip blocked candidates and briefly identify their referenced prerequisites.
3. Move the selected card to Refinement immediately and verify the move to signal that it has been picked up.
4. Begin [Refinement](02_refinement.md) in the same task. Existing clear requirements may let it advance immediately.

Moving a card into Approved does not itself trigger execution. Dependencies must be explicit, with issue/work-item references and the condition that unblocks the card. A task branch is required before implementation; no branch or worktree creation is required merely to approve or research a card.
